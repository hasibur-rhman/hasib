Ongoing Undergraduate Thesis 2026 – 2027

RainLite-YOLO: A Physics-Guided Feature-Space Progressive Deraining & Lightweight Object Detection Network

Domain: Autonomous Vehicles in Adverse Weather Conditions

Autonomous driving vision systems face catastrophic failure under severe rainy weather due to light scattering, refraction, and streak occlusions. RainLite-YOLO introduces a novel physics-guided formulation that decouples rain streak degradation in feature space rather than naive pixel restoration, followed by progressive deraining and lightweight object detection backbones.

Accurate Brain Tumor Detection using Swin Transformer

Developed a multi-stage hierarchical Swin Transformer for 4-class MRI brain tumor classification, achieving 93% accuracy. Leveraged shifted window self-attention (W-MSA) and patch merging modules with Automatic Mixed Precision (AMP) and Cosine Annealing learning rate optimization.
